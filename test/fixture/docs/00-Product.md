# Product

`greet` is a shell script that prints a greeting or a farewell to whoever
runs it, in English or Portuguese, quietly or shouting. It exists so the
focus-kit test harness has a project small enough to build a delivery in
one minute and real enough to have a verify command.

Audience: the person running the harness. Not a product anyone installs.

Out of scope: anything beyond `sh` and a handful of options.
